Most apps only tell you whether a sign is right or wrong.
QuickSign shows you why.

Every sign is scored on four parameters independently, so when you miss, you know exactly what to fix.

  • ✓Handshape
  • ✓Palm orientation
  • ✓Location
  • ✓Movement

Movement is checked over a ~2 second window, so you can't fake it by holding still.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. QuickSign runs in your mobile browser and can be installed as an app (PWA) on iOS and Android home screens.

Is my camera data uploaded?

No. Sign recognition runs entirely on-device using MediaPipe, so your video is never uploaded, recorded, or sent to a server.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start learning immediately as a guest. An account is only needed to sync progress across devices or use leaderboards and friends.

Is it free?

Yes, QuickSign is free to use.

Where should a total beginner start?

Most learners start with the ASL alphabet to get comfortable with handshapes, then move into the Say Hello world for your first conversational signs.

Was the sign content checked by Deaf ASL signers?

Not yet — we're a small student team and this is an open beta. Sign accuracy review by Deaf and hard-of-hearing signers is a known gap we want to close. If that's you, we'd genuinely value your feedback.

Help shape QuickSign

We're a small student team actively collecting feedback from Reddit and Discord testers. If something's broken or confusing, we want to hear about it.
